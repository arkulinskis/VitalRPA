The beacon: lighter than your favicon

Monitoring that slows the site down defeats the point. The VitalRPA beacon is a few hundred bytes of inline code that starts after your page has loaded.

Questions

Do I have to install anything?

No. Uptime, response time, keyword, certificate, domain and security-header checks all run from our servers without touching your site. The beacon is optional and only needed for visitors, page speed and JavaScript errors.

Will the snippet slow my site down?

No. It's about 800 bytes of inline code, so there's no extra file to download. It does nothing until your page has finished loading, then sends one background ping with navigator.sendBeacon, which the browser handles after everything else.

What does the beacon collect?

The page path, the site the visitor came from, the screen width, how long the page took to load, and up to 3 JavaScript error messages per page. No cookies, nothing stored in the browser, and it respects Do Not Track. Unique visitors are counted with a fingerprint whose secret changes every day; IP addresses are never stored.

How often do the robots check?

Every site has its own rhythm: every 5, 15 or 30 minutes, hourly (24 check-ins a day, the default) or less often. While a site is down, the robots look every 5 minutes so you know the moment it's back.

How will I hear about problems?

Through playbooks. Every new account starts with "email me when something breaks, and when it's fixed". Add webhooks for Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord or Zapier, a morning digest, or a second-robot recheck before anyone is paged.

Can I try it before adding my own site?

Yes. Sign up and you land in a sample fleet: eight fictional sites with outages, slow pages, an expiring certificate and a late backup, so you can see every feature working. Add your first site and the console becomes yours.

Is it free?

Yes, during early access: up to 50 sites and 50 heartbeats per account.
